# Asset delegation protocol

This protocol governs bounded native-asset delegation. The next experiment uses **Sol 6.1/high for orchestration and the author–verifier loop, with one reused Astra/high art director for the opening construction prototype, representation decisions and final finish**. Quality remains the acceptance condition; efficiency is an observation, not permission to lower the bar. This is a workflow experiment, not a model capability benchmark. The [Matriarch native trial](assets/native-matriarch-trial.md) completed under this role split; its [later refinement](assets/native-matriarch-refinement.md) stopped unfinished. Those records own the outcomes. This protocol includes subsequent guardrail changes and grants no new asset-execution authorization.

The [rulebook](art-direction.md) owns visual quality and [migration contract](migration.md) owns source/delivery/publication boundaries. Historical Storm Roc, Prowler and Wolf trials retain their original roles, limits, receipts and outcomes; see the [batch retrospective](assets/native-batch-1.md) and [Wolf forms record](assets/wolf-evolutions.md). Do not rewrite historical ledgers to fit this protocol.

## Roles and authority

| Role                             | Assignment                                                | Authority and exclusions                                                                                                                                                                   |
| -------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Trial orchestrator               | Root **gpt-6.1-sol / high**                               | Coordinates ownership, scheduling, compact handoffs, exceptions and budget decisions from reviewer receipts. Routes visual decisions to Astra; cannot substitute its aesthetic acceptance. |
| Art director                     | One fresh **gpt-6-astra / high** child, reused throughout | Owns scope, representation, opening native prototype and final source/consumer acceptance. May perform bounded final polish. Its final review is not independent of its own construction.  |
| Author                           | Fresh **gpt-6.1-sol / high** child                        | Develops the approved construction into editable source, rig/idle, meaningful native edit and frozen export evidence.                                                                      |
| Reviewer/operations (`verifier`) | Fresh **gpt-6.1-sol / high** child                        | Audits spec, independently reviews actual pixels, runs existing verification and accounting, then executes approved publication/cleanup/commit. Never edits candidate geometry.            |

Spawn exactly these three children with `fork_turns: none`, explicit model/effort and compact path ownership. Reuse them for the asset; no extra supervisors or helpers. Each worker must preserve concurrent edits. The same Astra context sees the baseline, prototype, escalations and finish; the independent Sol reviewer supplies the separate visual judgment. Declared role IDs are not authentication.

## Workflow and completion

1. **Pin the baseline and scope.** Inspect status and ownership first. Capture first-work UTC at the first attributable scope/baseline/spec work, before baseline preparation. Author prepares the existing whole-creature delivery and matched front/side/rear clay/material captures using the existing harness. Six views and a motion extreme suffice unless a named evidence gap needs detail. This assignment prepares the preserved baseline only. Astra inspects actual pixels, names a genuine visual/deformation defect and protects the good geometry. Initialize once baseline bytes can be pinned, preserving the original timestamp and intervening usage. Freeze identity/gesture, dominant defect, permitted representation, anatomical/motion interfaces, reference evidence, owned paths, exclusions, a meaningful native edit and **measured delivery limits**. Native edit verification must exercise the anatomy being developed, including fitted dependents; an unrelated inherited appendage control is insufficient.
2. **Audit and prove construction.** Sol reviewer audits feasibility, ambiguity, interfaces and observable acceptance of the exact spec hash. Start one bounded package before candidate geometry work. Astra makes an editable native prototype of the hardest transition, visible within whole-creature clay, a diagnostic detail and a motion pose. Preserve a separate frozen prototype snapshot; do not author over its pinned bytes. This is enough anatomy to demonstrate construction, not a polished whole asset. Record the `prototype` gate with source/capture hashes and rationale. No Sol author dispatch until it passes. For a family, prove one representative before repeating its construction across variants.
3. **Run the author–verifier loop.** Sol author refines within owned paths and approved representation, inspecting whole-creature clay and motion throughout. Export a frozen candidate with source/delivery/capture hashes; keep self-assessment separate. Reviewer inspects actual pixels **before** that self-assessment, then runs applicable native/export/browser gates. Every failure names **view / visible defect / cause / correction / success evidence**, classified as structural, surface or technical. Technical passes cannot excuse visibly detached, inflated or poorly fitted anatomy. The **first structural failure** requires Astra's recorded representation diagnosis before another author repair. Central construction replacement is recorded as **takeover**. Ordinary surface/technical repairs stay in the existing loop, within the same counters.
4. **Finish under Astra.** After independent clearance, Astra inspects exact source and consumer pixels. It may accept or record `reopen` before bounded final native polish. Reopening retains the original package clock/failures, invalidates clearance/acceptance and requires fresh export and independent review of changed bytes. A rejection of required criteria increments failures; optional finishing does not. Substantial reconstruction is takeover, not successful Sol middle execution. Astra's prototype and polish happen **before** the final-review reserve. Normal author/reviewer clay and motion checks remain required; add Astra review for substantial shape changes or concrete concerns.
5. **Accept exact bytes and close out.** Astra records actual final acceptance of source, delivery and evidence through the existing release review (`reviewer.role: parent`, `reviewer.name` equal to the configured Astra director ID), then the ledger's `acceptance` gate pins that review and the cleared candidate manifest. Technical verification, independent clearance, art-direction acceptance and user approval remain distinct. Reviewer obtains the `publisher` handoff and executes publication, retention-authorized cleanup and an exact-owned-path semantic commit with LFS binaries; do not push. Return preview, visual verdict, verification, limitations, status and retrospective. Preserve originals, prior publication pointers and strongest unfinished candidates. Stop after the one asset.

Shared tooling stays frozen during the asset. Workers report a concise diagnosis and proposed fix to the orchestrator and Astra before any shared-tool change; perform approved infrastructure repair as a separately identified bounded task. Asset-local fixes stay within assigned ownership. Batch coherent operations, reuse pinned captures and repeat checks only for changed bytes, failures or a concrete unresolved concern.

## Cumulative limits

| Scope                                               | Stop before the next dispatch or revision at                        |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Each unresolved defect/package                      | **30 working minutes or 2 failed candidate revisions**              |
| Entire asset, all participants                      | **90 working minutes or 1,000 modeled Standard credit-equivalents** |
| Reserved for frozen-candidate final review/closeout | **20 minutes and 200 credits**                                      |

Prototype, authoring, repair and final polish share the same unresolved-defect package clock and failures. A new agent, spec, package label, representation or takeover never resets them. Two failures includes the first rejected candidate and its rejected repair. Estimate the whole next step, including coordination and changed-byte verification, before dispatch. A cheap worker call alone is insufficient. `start` and `reopen` estimates must leave the reserve and fit remaining package time. Neither reserve is an extra allowance; once either boundary is reached, no modeling or repair is permitted. If frozen-candidate review fails there, preserve an unfinished result.

New trials use **version 3** of the existing ledger for separated orchestration/art direction and construction/acceptance gates. Version 1 retains its historical 90/30/2/250 contract; version 2 retains its declared limits and three-role semantics. No migration of active or historical receipts is permitted. A material allowance increase needs explicit user authorization, keeps original totals and cannot revive a stopped trial. Do not introduce a 300-credit target or 150-credit overhead flag.

Working time is wall-clock minus explicitly evidenced user-wait intervals. Overlapping agents count once for elapsed time; all usage is summed. Rendering, tool waits and scheduling count. Separately report any sum of agent durations. At a cap freeze evidence, record the smallest useful disposition and stop authoring. Late evidence/usage remains recordable; do not repeatedly request extensions. A new spec requires re-audit and renewed prototype/clearance while original clocks and failures continue.

Reviewer/operations alone refreshes credits/status immediately before dispatch, repair and closeout; the orchestrator consumes that one fresh receipt. Preserve baseline/setup and infrastructure costs separately **and** in all-in comparisons. Report role/model/effort totals, cached/new input and output costs, elapsed time, cutoff, coverage and outstanding usage. Prowler's **610.58-credit checkpoint included setup and coordination**, not controlled marginal production cost. Wolf's direct and delegated phases also differed in inherited work and iteration opportunities. Assess whether quality held, where intervention was needed and where time/cost went; one trial cannot establish model superiority or strategy efficiency.

## Ledger and handoffs

Use [`trial.ts`](../../packages/workshop-tools/art/trial.ts), the existing collector and delivery pipeline. Receipts live in a new child of `.work/sessions/<session>/`, separate from candidate bytes. Paths resolve from repository root; use pinned Node. The parent directory must exist.

```sh
node packages/workshop-tools/art/trial.ts init .work/sessions/<session>/matriarch-trial config.json
node packages/workshop-tools/art/trial.ts credits .work/sessions/<session>/matriarch-trial <actual-root-thread-uuid>
node packages/workshop-tools/art/trial.ts record .work/sessions/<session>/matriarch-trial event.json
node packages/workshop-tools/art/trial.ts status .work/sessions/<session>/matriarch-trial
node packages/workshop-tools/art/trial.ts handoff .work/sessions/<session>/matriarch-trial auditor
```

`handoff` supports `auditor`, `author`, `verifier`, `director`, plus v3 `orchestrator` and `publisher`. Redirect the result into an ignored handoff file. `author` requires an audited spec and current pinned prototype; unresolved structural failure blocks it. `director` distinguishes native construction, representation diagnosis and final review. `verifier` after clearance prepares frozen-candidate closeout, without publication authority. `publisher` requires Astra acceptance of the latest cleared candidate under the current spec; it rechecks candidate/review pins before returning. Author handoffs recheck prototype source and capture pins. The publication pipeline still validates full evidence closure and current source/input freshness.

Status/credits exit **2** holds production; **1** means invalid data. At reserve, `allowed` is false while `reviewAllowed` can remain true. Final review, verification and accepted publication may continue on frozen bytes; geometry dispatch cannot. Actual caps, failure limits, stops, user waits and missing/stale coverage block all dispatch, including closeout. A refused handoff exits 1; evidence recording remains possible. Handoffs are checkpoint decisions, not runtime locks.

Initialize v3 with actual values; this example is not executable evidence:

```json
{
  "kind": "init",
  "version": 3,
  "trial": "matriarch-sol-astra-trial",
  "asset": "matriarch",
  "limits": { "assetMinutes": 90, "packageMinutes": 30, "failedRevisions": 2, "credits": 1000 },
  "reserve": { "minutes": 20, "credits": 200 },
  "at": "REPLACE_WITH_FIRST_WORK_UTC",
  "spec": "Replace with the audited shared specification, measured limits and reference paths.",
  "baseline": [{ "path": "path/to/baseline-manifest.json", "sha256": "REPLACE_WITH_REAL_SHA256" }],
  "roles": {
    "orchestrator": { "id": "ACTUAL_ROOT_THREAD_UUID", "model": "gpt-6.1-sol", "effort": "high" },
    "director": { "id": "ACTUAL_ASTRA_AGENT_ID", "model": "gpt-6-astra", "effort": "high" },
    "author": { "id": "ACTUAL_AUTHOR_AGENT_ID", "model": "gpt-6.1-sol", "effort": "high" },
    "verifier": { "id": "ACTUAL_VERIFIER_AGENT_ID", "model": "gpt-6.1-sol", "effort": "high" }
  }
}
```

All four IDs must differ. The v3 accounting root must equal `roles.orchestrator.id`, the actual working root thread UUID, not Astra's child ID. Models/effort are declarations; dispatch must select them explicitly. Baseline manifests pin source, delivery and matched captures; initialization verifies the manifest bytes. Retain all evidence it references.

Events contain `kind` and canonical UTC ISO `at` with milliseconds. Order is chronological. A pin is `{path, sha256}`. `specHash` is SHA-256 of the exact spec text (available in status even on telemetry hold).

| Kind               | Additional fields / behavior                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `spec`             | `text`, `reason`, `director`. Invalidates audit and v3 prototype/acceptance; does not reset counters.                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| `audit`            | `reviewer` verifier ID, `specHash`, `approved` boolean, `findings`, nonempty `evidence` list.                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| `usage`            | Cumulative `credits`, `through`, `coverage` (`complete` or `partial`), `source`; collector adds accounting identity/hashes.                                                                                                                                                                  |
| `start`            | Unique `package`, `defect`, `ownedPaths`, `interfaces`, `acceptance`; v2/v3 `estimate: {minutes, credits, basis}`. One active package. V3 initially assigns native work to director.                                                                                                         |
| `prototype` (v3)   | `package`, `director`, `specHash`, `source` pin, `views: {whole, detail, motion}` pins, `rationale`. Transfers native work to author; source/capture bytes checked.                                                                                                                          |
| `review`           | Active `package`, `reviewer` verifier ID, `specHash`, `candidate` manifest pin, `verdict` (`clear` or `fail`), nonempty `evidence`, `critique`. V3 failures also require `classification` (`structural`, `surface`, `technical`), `cause`, `correction`, `successEvidence`.                  |
| `direction` (v3)   | After structural failure: `package`, `director`, `specHash`, `diagnosis`, `operation`, `successEvidence`, nonempty `evidence`, `action` (`repair` or `takeover`). Clears escalation; takeover assigns geometry to director and remains recorded.                                             |
| `reopen` (v3)      | Latest cleared `package`, `director`, `specHash`, `mode` (`polish` or `takeover`), `rejected` boolean, `reason`, nonempty `evidence`, `estimate`. Invalidates clearance/acceptance and assigns geometry to director. `rejected: true` increments failures. Original package clock continues. |
| `acceptance` (v3)  | Latest cleared `package`, `director`, `specHash`, exact `candidate` pin, accepted release `review` pin, `verdict` text. Checks declared Astra review identity and candidate digest; does not authenticate the writer or replace pipeline validation.                                         |
| `pause` / `resume` | Pause `reason` must be `user-wait`, with `evidence` describing pending input. Resume closes that wait.                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| `intervention`     | `actor`, `model`, `effort`, `detail`, nonempty `evidence`. Record reassignment and material corrections; never resets gates/counters.                                                                                                                                                        |
| `stop`             | `reason`, nonempty `evidence`. Permanently denies handoffs while retaining late evidence.                                                                                                                                                                                                    |

An explicit user-authorized increase on v2/v3 uses append-only `allowance`: `director`, strictly increased `credits`, positive `reserveCredits` below cap, `authorization` quoting that decision, nonempty `evidence`. Only credits/reserve change, never clocks, failures or stops. V1 cannot use it.

`init` refuses an existing directory; `record` writes exclusive numbered hash-linked receipts. Preserve/back up the chain. It detects accidental edits/reordering, not tail deletion or impersonation; it is not a signed audit log. Likewise, the existing release field `reviewer.role: parent` is a compatibility label for art-direction acceptance, not proof that the root orchestrator reviewed or that a particular model authored the record. Operations may serialize Astra's actual decision, with its exact ID and evidence, but cannot self-declare it. Legacy v1/v2 handoffs retain their original parent-director behavior.

## Cost evidence and enforcement limits

**Use the CLI; do not research rates or write a new accounting script during asset work.** `trial.ts credits DIR WORKING_THREAD_UUID` reads the ledger's original start, discovers the local working thread and its descendant agents, applies the pinned rate card, writes an exclusive usage report and appends a cumulative `usage` event. Reviewer/operations runs it at the checkpoints above; it exits 2 if coverage or the trial gate is blocked. A successful collection never bypasses the time, cost or failure caps. The report identifies source paths, exact line numbers, response IDs, file-prefix hashes, model/effort and per-thread totals without message bodies or private reasoning. The ledger pins both report bytes and rate-card bytes. Repeating collection recomputes the cumulative total; it does not add the previous snapshot again.

For a read-only report without a ledger, use:

```sh
node packages/workshop-tools/art/credits.ts --root <working-thread-uuid> --since <asset-start-ISO> --out <new-report.json>
```

`--through ISO` freezes a historical cutoff; `--sessions-root DIR` uses an explicit log fixture/root. Defaults are `$CODEX_HOME/sessions` and its existing `archived_sessions` sibling, or `~/.codex` when unset. The collector indexes metadata, then streams only the selected lineage at bounded file sizes. It deduplicates by owning thread and response ID, excludes inherited usage from other threads and cumulative `token_count` totals, and includes reasoning tokens only through the already inclusive output counter. Model changes use their corresponding rates. Entire responses recorded at or after the start count, including boundary-straddling responses; there is no invented timestamp proration.

Freshness means **the logs were scanned within 120 seconds**, not that an idle agent must generate another usage record. `through` is the collection cutoff; `lastUsageAt` is reported separately. `complete` means the discovered, persisted per-response records reconcile with supported counters/rates and no detected coverage gaps. It does **not** mean a live response has already reported its usage. Open task markers are shown as possible in-flight turns, not proof of active execution. This checkpoint lag is an explicit overrun risk, not grounds for repeatedly querying models just to create fresh telemetry. The dispatcher still accounts for all participants; unrelated external helpers, missing undiscoverable logs and external charges require separate reconciliation. Scaffold work outside the trial remains separately identified in the all-in strategy assessment; repairs incurred during an active trial stay in its cumulative history.

Unknown model rates, conflicting duplicates, malformed/truncated logs, missing discovered child logs, unsupported counters and detected unmetered image calls produce named gaps and `partial` coverage. Missing, partial or stale coverage holds dispatch; unknown cost is not zero. The collector returns the known subtotal with those gaps. Preserve its report and request a bounded scaffold correction here; do not begin another web-search/accounting loop. If a previously recorded manual total exceeds the collected total, the ledger refuses a decrease: reconcile its evidence rather than resetting it. Directly recorded manual receipts remain available for evidenced external charges, with their scope disclosed.

The checked [rate card](../../packages/workshop-tools/art/credit-rates.json) is a frozen **2026-10-08** snapshot of the [official Standard credit rates](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/pricing). Astra is **250 / 25 / 1250**, Sol 6.1 **50 / 2.5 / 250** per million uncached input / cached input / output tokens. The strategy/scaffold owner maintains this card when the accounting contract changes; routine runs perform no web request. A ledger refuses a changed rate-card hash or root identity without explicit reconciliation. This trial's denominator is normalized **Standard credit-equivalents** across speed modes: the tool does not apply Fast/Ultrafast billing multipliers or claim actual purchased credits, subscription quota, invoices or final billed cost.

The tool enforces a checkpoint decision, not a runtime scheduler. In-flight tools and responses can exceed a limit; report the overrun and dispatch no further authoring work. The CLI cannot kill agents, observe unlogged interventions, prevent manual edits or guarantee that a consumer obeys its exit code. Session receipts are ignored working data and require explicit validation; maintained tests exercise synthetic receipts. Ledger closure does not authorize deletion of source, baseline or review evidence; [retention governance](../engineering/maintenance.md) still applies.

## Opening construction rejections

Use `prototype-rejection` for a formal failed opening construction, before a successful
prototype handoff or author-candidate review. The director or independent verifier pins
`package`, `reviewer`, current `specHash`, `source`, `views` (`whole`, `detail`, `motion`),
`critique`, `cause`, `correction` and `successEvidence`. The existing CLI verifies those
source/capture hashes before recording the receipt. The event is available to v2/v3
ledgers; it adds one failure to the original unresolved package without resetting time,
changing allowances or granting author handoff. Existing historical events keep their
meaning and are not rewritten.

A formally rejected initial construction and its formally rejected repair consume the
existing two-failure allowance. Routine internal edits without a formal review decision
are not automatically separate failures. Record rejection when it happens; freezing an
unfinished result with zero author reviews must not conceal earlier formal construction
rejections. After a successful construction proof, use the existing candidate review or
final-review reopening path instead. Apply the [structural review criteria](art-direction.md#structural-rejection-versus-surface-finishing) before surface work.
