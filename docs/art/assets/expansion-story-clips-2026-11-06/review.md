# One Light Left Behind · review and limitations

Candidate fiction grounded in Night Passage's Root Sepulcher slate, pale stone,
roots and bronze lanterns. The traveler and encounter are not accepted canon.
Existing movie trials and static game art cannot supply these narrative actions;
this is a bounded story composition, with no runtime slot/trigger/timing consumer.

- **Reference:** Connected hooded traveler, grounded boots and readable lantern/
  sconce/root doorway at native/compact sizes. The generated sconce is a cup,
  so the later action places the lantern base rather than hanging its handle.
- **Raise:** Deliberate lantern lift, stable identity. Residual motion remains
  during the requested final hold; SVG edges soften in GIF rendering.
- **Offer:** Visible placement, release and empty hand. Sconce geometry changes
  toward a bracket/support; exact fixture geometry is not preserved.
- **Answer:** Thick roots bend toward the right jamb, opening the threshold.
  Root motion is stylized; stone/background edges drift.
- **Pass:** Alternating steps and delayed cloak motion read. The late silhouette
  fades into darkness rather than using occlusion alone. Only the final two
  frames are fully empty/still, fewer than five requested.
- **Joins:** Palette, scale and edges visibly jump. This is a connected narrative,
  not seamless continuous footage or accepted production animation.

All64 original frames/timings remain unchanged in the source and joined GIFs.
No frame was dropped or repainted. Technical validity and author self-review do
not establish user aesthetic/canon acceptance or runtime compatibility.
