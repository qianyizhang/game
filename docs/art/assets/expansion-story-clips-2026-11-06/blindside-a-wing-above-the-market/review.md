# Review — A Wing Above the Market

Author candidate assessment: useful two-beat watch-to-departure story, with visible
redraw and stylized-anatomy limits. Technical success is not user acceptance.

The source Nightjar's ink/coral/ochre identity, pale eye and tapering tail survive
in the new wide composition. The SVG has two visibly supported feet and readable
connected head/neck/breast. At 160×112 the small bird remains recognizable; the
quiet Watch action is subtler than the later airborne change. The moon is fully
in frame (32 px top clearance in the actual opening PNG); an initial concern
about its top crop was a preview misread, corrected before the final dispatch.

Watch keeps both attached feet on the same rail in all 16 frames. The folded
coral wing stays connected. The skyward attention is readable, but the head rises
about 30 px and neck/breast elongate: this is visible posture/proportion drift,
not constant scale or merely a tiny head rotation. The GIF redraw also changes
placement and texture from the SVG. Its exact last composed frame was the sole
PNG pose input for Away; no unverified GIF input was used.

Away shows supported feet at frames 0–2, a push/release transition at 3, and clear
sky under the feet by 4. One connected bird opens near/far wings, makes a broad
beat and finishes airborne right/up of the vacated perch. Every actual frame was
inspected at native 480×336 and compact 160×112, with native GIF playback. No
head, tail, foot or wingtip leaves the frame. The card remains untouched. Wing
planes/feather counts simplify and the silhouette stretches through the turn;
this is stylized motion rather than validated species locomotion. Flight goes
farther/higher than the plan and the final beak approaches the moon, without an
observed overlap or crop. These remain review limits, not silent repairs.

The chapter cut shifts the bird, rail, card and moon. The join deliberately
preserves every frame and timing; no smoothing, omission, interpolation, retouch,
extra holds or crossfade conceals the cut. Both source GIFs remain byte-for-byte
API originals with their metadata. The assembled GIF is clearly a local edit,
with copied source-attestation metadata stripped from that derivative only.

The HTML review uses native GIF playback and an SVG reduced-motion fallback.
It claims no pause/seek controls. The shared gallery has a known Stop→Play restart
failure after a nonlooping joined GIF ends; this lane does not change that shared
implementation or claim repeated playback works. Initial playback and a direct
new page load are separate from that limitation.

The rail, decorative face-down card, watch and departure are proposed fiction.
No rank/suit/face value is established, so this story does not depict or change
the actual Nightjar held-face-card multiplier. No runtime trigger, effect or
timing consumer is invented. Canonical registration is still a draft blocker.
