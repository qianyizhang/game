1. **Watch:** feet remain visibly supported on the rail;head inclines toward the

2. **Away:** begin from the actual first endpoint. Two connected wings open;

   camera chase,complete frame exit,second bird or fading substitute.

outer primaries may separate at tips rather than form detached feather fans.
