## Phoenix baseline and scope

The [preserved delivery](../../../packages/dcc-workbench/references/phoenix/legacy-gallery.glb) and [provenance](../../../packages/dcc-workbench/references/phoenix/provenance.json) freeze the current Phoenix. Its asymmetric raised wings, upright gesture, feather hierarchy, tail sweep and supported stance are the comparison, not a mandate to reuse Nightjar's perching-wing construction. Working scripts and exact source snapshots remain under `.work/sessions/native-batch-1-2026-10-08/phoenix/`; the TypeScript source and SVG remain preserved.

Baseline measurements are **294,140 triangles, 12,802,280 GLB bytes, 504 meshes and three unique joints**. The file has 169 skins sharing those joints; 507 summed joint references do not mean 507 distinct bones. It carries one six-second idle.

Before authoring, the parent set delivery limits of **350,000 triangles, 20,000,000 bytes and 16 joints per skin** around that measured baseline. They permit selective refinement without forcing destructive global decimation. These are subject-specific desktop allowances; physical-phone performance remains unmeasured. A reason to exceed a cap needs an explicit parent decision and new evidence, not a quiet threshold change.

The candidate must retain editable native anatomy, packed materials, rig and fitted idle and demonstrate one useful semantic edit through restoration, save/reload, export and browser agreement. The imported source has one armature with three bones, 169 skinned meshes, 335 rigid meshes and seven packed images. The **Fan lift** control on `EDIT | Wing.1` affects the lower extended wing while anchoring its proximal 15 cm. Values **0, 0.5 and 1** carry the mantle, front/rear coverts, secondary vanes and shafts, wrist and primary fan through a six-degree distal arc. The farthest tip moves about **13 cm**; the body, face, opposite wing, grip and basalt remain fixed. It is a static native authoring edit alongside the saved idle, not a delivered morph animation.

Matched clay and material reviews identified exposed square lower-wing roots, a weak shoulder transition, a separated claw cap and opposing toes buried in the basalt. Sleeve and separate plate approaches were rejected. After those revisions repeated the same visual failure, the parent directly authored a joined mantle, buried greater-covert roots and shallow surface-fitted lesser coverts. Inspection also identified an inherited covert row still exposing its cut roots in front of replacement layers; that redundant row was removed. Original primary and secondary flight vanes, shafts, body, opposite wing and asymmetric gesture were preserved. The artist separately repaired visible toe paths and six fitted claw hooks while retaining the stance and basalt.

The combined construction passed the parent's scoped review for native-control authoring. Its frozen trial source is `phoenix-construction-merged-p6.blend`, SHA-256 `3aad290c20b3f87e47cde128f48f42bcb582156148dbf6ef0ef68b8baed38efe`, with **316,412 triangles**. The six-claw merge preserved the other **477 delivery meshes**. Whole and enlarged front/side/rear clay, material and support evidence remain in the protected batch session and review roots. The artist also inspected the parent-authored result before control work. This is guided Sol work with substantial parent modeling intervention, not an unaided author result. The subsequent control and delivery gates are recorded below.

Residual shallow feather scalloping, the inherited atlas pattern and small angular toe-to-claw transitions remain explicit stylization limits. Rejected sources and captures are preserved; the parent accepted the exact final candidate for gallery publication after native and exported clay/material/contact/motion review. User art approval remains unrecorded.
