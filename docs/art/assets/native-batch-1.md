## Phoenix baseline and scope

The [preserved delivery](../../../packages/dcc-workbench/references/phoenix/legacy-gallery.glb) and [provenance](../../../packages/dcc-workbench/references/phoenix/provenance.json) freeze the current Phoenix. Its asymmetric raised wings, upright gesture, feather hierarchy, tail sweep and supported stance are the comparison, not a mandate to reuse Nightjar's perching-wing construction. Working scripts and exact source snapshots remain under `.work/sessions/native-batch-1-2026-10-08/phoenix/`; the TypeScript source and SVG remain preserved.

Baseline measurements are **294,140 triangles, 12,802,280 GLB bytes, 504 meshes and three unique joints**. The file has 169 skins sharing those joints; 507 summed joint references do not mean 507 distinct bones. It carries one six-second idle.

Before authoring, the parent set delivery limits of **350,000 triangles, 20,000,000 bytes and 16 joints per skin** around that measured baseline. They permit selective refinement without forcing destructive global decimation. These are subject-specific desktop allowances; physical-phone performance remains unmeasured. A reason to exceed a cap needs an explicit parent decision and new evidence, not a quiet threshold change.

The candidate must retain editable native anatomy, packed materials, rig and fitted idle and demonstrate one useful semantic edit through restoration, save/reload, export and browser agreement. The imported source has one armature with three bones, 169 skinned meshes, 335 rigid meshes and seven packed images. The proposed **Fan lift** edit affects the lower extended wing while anchoring the shoulder root and carrying fitted feather dependents; its usable range and exact probes remain under construction. Parent review of matched front/side/rear clay and material retained the asymmetric gesture but requested a small repair over exposed square lower-wing feather roots and the weak shoulder/leading-edge coverage. An independent enlarged grip review also found a separated claw cap and opposing toes buried in the basalt; the requested repair preserves the stance and rock while reconnecting and fitting the digits. Contact measurements alone do not certify that fit. Two sleeve/band approaches failed the leading-edge review: the second covered the roots but read as a smooth rail and wrist cuff. The parent stopped that representation and specified hidden thin support with staggered shallow covert feathers, keeping the outer flight contour fixed. Rejected sources and captures are preserved. Final control and delivery acceptance remain pending. A file-format conversion alone is not completion.
