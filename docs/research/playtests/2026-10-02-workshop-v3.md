# Workshop v3 development playtests

Checked 2026-10-02. All three engines use rules version **3** and pin content manifests. These are legal-command lifecycle tests with unmodified normal-run resources, not estimates of human difficulty or fun.

## Method

`npm run playtest` completes eight Blindside runs, twelve Spire runs (both characters, A0 and A5) and twelve Last Hearth lobbies. Every exported replay must reconstruct the exact final state. Hearth also checks every recruitable definition’s pool accounting after every accepted human action and resolved bot round.

Spire’s policy uses one-command lookahead and can observe consequences of hidden draws. Blindside evaluates visible hands and sampled deck composition. Hearth uses inspectable recruitment heuristics. The cohorts test these particular policies and seeds. Blindside’s policy currently buys ordinary stock and planets; focused rule/browser tests, rather than its cohort, exercise new packs, vouchers and skips.

## Portable evidence

The old generated v3 cohort directories were retired during [lean cleanup](../../engineering/history/cleanup-triage.md). This table is the dated result; selected tracked fixtures and the maintained simulation tests remain. New playtest output is a fresh run, not the original retained evidence.

Generated folders contain each command replay, a summary and an `evidence.json` file with source `automated`, content manifests, choices and encounters. Preserve a copy before rerunning. Use `npm run playtest:compare -- baseline.json candidate.json` for matched-seed comparisons; the report retains both manifests and excludes incomplete or ambiguous pairs.

The tracked browser fixtures are legal histories selected from this cohort: Blindside MOD-02/MOD-01, Spire IRONCLAD-06/IRONCLAD-04, and Hearth HEARTH-11/HEARTH-03 for victory/defeat. Browser tests use their real prefixes for late-game interactions. They do not click every action of all 32 runs.

## Human questions

- Does the visible boss change route and drafting decisions? Does A5 attrition make rest versus upgrade meaningful?
- Are Silent Poison, Shivs and discard engines understandable without prior game knowledge?
- Do playback speed and pause controls explain timing without making routine turns tedious?
- Are blind skip rewards worth losing a payout and shop? Does scouting change a recruitment choice?
- When a mod alters pick rates, is the denominator comparable and are the same play modes/content versions being compared?
