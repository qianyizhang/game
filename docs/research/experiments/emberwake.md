## Shared interpretation of the later screens

- Official performance2 is `[won,bosses,wards,regions,won ? executedTicks : 0]`:
  maximize the first four components lexicographically, then minimize winning
  **executed native ticks**. Decisions, packed commands and requested replay padding
  are different measures. Nonwins get no time bonus. Farmable resources and free
  portal healing do not earn rank bonuses.
- For analysis only, equally complete victories practically tie when
  `20 * abs(tA - tB) < max(tA,tB)`. Exact 5% stays directional; this changes no native
  score. Net material superiority is `(better-worse)/all blocks`, not percentage
  speedup or victory-rate gain.
- D9–D14 use consumed `EMBERWAKE-COHERENT-POLICY3-DEV-01..10`: Immediate/Survival 180
  plus Growth 18 on the first two blocks; all 27 hero/tier/style cells remain. They
  use 8,000 decisions, `POLICY-STREAM-1`, empty options, pool 4; 2,048 MiB V8 old
  generation per worker and 150,000,000-byte encoded **and** decoded gzip limits.
  Nine material contrasts each require ≥ 20 pp over all ten pairs, six completion
  floors each require 8/10, and three Severe-existence guards each require ≥ 1/12.
  There is no significance/population claim at n=10. Deaths, ties and living stops
  remain; errors, missing cases, failed audits or source drift block integrity.
- Native6/7 compare 132 unchanged-tier journals/full states after only declared
  replay format, replay rulesVersion and state rulesVersion normalization.
  Policy9–12 compare 108 original policy8/native7 Immediate/Growth journals/full states
  **exactly, without normalization**. Reused controls are not fresh baseline
  execution, identical raw traces or equal-compute evidence.
- Later independent reviews reconstruct all 198 original-input/native cases and
  fixed 27 DEV01 factory cases; the other 171 factory verdicts remain
  producer-qualified. Full producer audits are distinct from full independent
  factory reproduction. Technical worker copies and local branches stay outside
  study denominators. Sampled process-wide RSS may miss peaks and is not the V8
  heap setting or an RSS ceiling. No human-equivalent perception/difficulty,
  enjoyment, hostile-code isolation or universal strategy ordering follows.
