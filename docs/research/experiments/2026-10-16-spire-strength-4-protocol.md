# Spire strength-4 confirmation protocol · 2026-10-16

This prospective design is not an executed result. Development is still open. The
candidate becomes frozen only when its complete source manifest and this protocol
are retained before the first reserved attempt. No strength-4 reserved outcomes have
been generated or inspected. Changing the candidate after that freeze requires a new
version, protocol and reserved namespace.

## Evidence and development

Both previous confirmations failed their original promotion criteria; their protocols,
source snapshots and original verdicts remain unchanged. Strength-2 Ironclad Growth
won 6/60 at A0 versus 2/60 historically. Strength-3 Ironclad Survival won 12/100 versus
0/100, but its Wilson lower bound was below 10%. Silent passed the statistical criteria
in both studies. Three historical-control budget timeouts also failed strength-3's
strict all-terminal requirement.

Those consumed outcomes inform development only. Version 4 repairs traced hand-end
HP exposure, resource valuation, lethal-target debuff credit and ordered public enemy
status effects. Survival Ironclad also curates rewards, affordable purchases and
campfire upgrades using public priorities adapted from the existing simulation
heuristic, without its privileged combat lookahead. Native rule meaning remains unchanged. Complete development evidence,
including rejected experiments and unstable small-sample gains, must accompany the
final candidate. Increasing sample size improves precision; it does not improve policy
strength. Development results do not establish confirmation success.

## Representative levels chosen before the A3 development probe

The production candidate's completed 40-block development run at 0/2/5 gave Ironclad
9/40, 12/40 and 3/40 wins; Silent gave 16/40, 10/40 and 5/40. Ironclad's inversion
is uncertain: 10 A2-only versus 7 A0-only wins, paired two-sided exact p = 0.629.
Extra elite rooms also change reward opportunities; these data do not establish
that A2 helps this policy.

For mechanical coverage, this prospective study selects **0/3/5**: A3 strengthens
elite fights as well as normal enemies. A2 already affects Slaver elite-pack members
and Slime Boss's normal-kind children, so it is not a pure normal-only intervention.
This middle-level choice is declared **before the full A3 development outcomes**.
The candidate stays fixed for an A3 probe on all forty existing development blocks
for both characters. If ordering fails, retain that result; do not search successive
middle levels until one passes. All original A2 studies and labels remain intact.

**Completed A3 probe, before reserved freeze:** Ironclad won **12/40**, Silent
**9/40**. All eighty attempts terminated without execution errors or source drift;
original-input rollouts and exact source/bundle bytes are retained. These development
screens do not have complete independent audit coverage. Ironclad's selected 0/3/5
point estimates are therefore **9/40, 12/40, 3/40**: the middle-order criterion
**currently fails in development**. Silent's are 16/40, 9/40 and 5/40. No further
middle level is substituted. The fixed fresh confirmation will test the declared
criteria; these results do not warrant promising overall calibration success.

Only the intermediate point changes prospectively; strength and A0–A5 effect-size,

**development-selected representative levels**, not a demonstrated ordering of every

- **Population:** 400 independent blocks, `SPIRE-STRENGTH-4-RESERVED-01..400`;
  Ironclad and Silent separately, each at Ascensions 0/3/5. Every attempt uses
  `POLICY-STREAM-1` and a fixed budget of 2,000 decisions.
- **Primary:** Survival `spire-policy-4` and exact historical Survival
  `spire-policy-1` for both characters: **4,800 attempts**. Historical control factory
  comes from retained bundle
  `ebb2e5a27cd1591d94ff2ddf354900ea120b9913c7860b829fb254e86b8bbb47` through a
  source-pinned local module and the current native adapter.
- **Companion:** Immediate and Growth v4 on the first twenty identical blocks and
  six setups: **240 attempts**. Compare strategies only on those matched twenty
  blocks. Companion outcomes cannot change the primary reference.
- **Identity:** native rules 3, observation `spire-observation-1`, score
  `spire-progress-1`, base content version 3/digest `8192b770`. Primary and companion
  must match this exact contract, population, setup, stream and budget.
- **Freeze:** retain all native/policy/module/tool/analysis source bytes, complete
  source hashes, compiled bundles, original protocol and exact population before
  execution. Preserve every original input, proposal/work count, replay and audit.
  Missing/duplicate cases, source drift or incomplete provenance prevent promotion.
- **Fixed sample:** no optional stopping, tuning on partial outcomes or post-hoc
  expansion. Four hundred blocks improve precision near the earlier 12–15% Ironclad
  A0 rates; they do not guarantee meeting any criterion.

## Unchanged four statistical criteria

The primary family is two A0 v4-versus-v1 strength contrasts and two v4 A0-versus-A5
contrasts, one per character. Use exact two-sided conditional binomial tests on
paired discordant wins and Holm correction across these four contrasts; report
individual 95% Wilson intervals and approximate paired Student t progress intervals.
Paired binary intervals remain unavailable.

- **Strength per character:** A0 Wilson lower bound >10%, observed improvement over
  historical Survival >=10 percentage points, Holm-adjusted p <0.05.
- **Difficulty per character:** A0–A5 win-rate gap >=10 percentage points,
  Holm-adjusted p <0.05, and A3's point estimate between A0 and A5.
- **Scope:** Holm controls this study's four tests only. It does not adjust the
  repeated, adaptively developed confirmation campaign across studies 2/3/4. Retain
  every failed study and report this selection limit. No adjacent-level significance,
  human difficulty or equal-compute strategy ranking is claimed.

## Prospective bounded endpoint and operational integrity

The endpoint is **victory within 2,000 decisions**. Every budget timeout counts as a
nonwin for **both candidate and historical control**, stays in its paired denominator,
and remains fully inspectable. This prospective correction distinguishes a measured
failure to finish from invalid execution data. It does not reinterpret either prior
study's stricter all-terminal protocol or change native game rules.

All **5,040 attempts** must exist and pass integrity, replay, permitted-input and
matching-policy reproduction audits. Execution errors, failed/unavailable audits,
wrong identities, incomplete grids and source drift block promotion. A timeout with
a winning metric is inconsistent and also blocks promotion. Report native terminal
completion and every budget timeout separately; bounded calibration does not assert
that every campaign terminates naturally.

The analyzer writes a separate inference artifact with exclusive creation. Summary
validation alone is insufficient: an independent review must inspect raw artifacts,
recompute summaries, verify retained source bytes and reproduce selected original
inputs with their pinned bundle. Strategy/work comparisons on twenty matched blocks
remain descriptive. Failure remains failure.
