## Candidate and rationale

Native6 changes **Standard incoming damage from ×8 to ×5**, uniformly for all heroes.
Relaxed remains ×0.75 and Severe ×12. Player ceilings remain unlimited / 55% / 70%
of current maximum HP; health-potion recovery remains 0 / 20 / 60 seconds.
Policy8, observation3, score2, content, control intervals, budgets, enemy HP,
telegraphs, seeded randomness and legal refuge behavior remain unchanged.

Consumed native5 journals show that Barbarian and Sorceress rely on refuge HP
restoration more than Necromancer. A general healing limit or enemy reset therefore
risks removing existing Severe viability. Standard relief is the smaller balance
change and preserves Severe behavior. For a source-only breakpoint illustration,
an elite impact of `13 × 1.45 × 1.35` deals an uncapped ally 203.58 damage at factor8
and 127.2375 at factor5: a 200-HP ally changes from one fatal impact to surviving
that impact. This arithmetic is not an alternative campaign or a calibration claim.

No new healing resource, hero-specific handicap, score component, practical time
tolerance, policy strategy or reference selection is introduced. Native5 and older
campaigns must retain exact original rules, replay headers, saves and continuations.
The original native5 source/bundle was frozen before any native6 edit.
Nine fixed seed01 native5 Survival recordings supply historical continuity
witnesses only. Their native-prefix probes are privileged correctness evidence;
they do not select pressure values or provide new policy-strength evidence.

## Development gates

All gates below are fixed before case1. They are descriptive development screens,
not population inference or substitutes for the later fresh protocol.

- **Integrity:** all 198 declared attempts and four audit dimensions complete;
  no execution errors, source drift, identity mismatch or unaccounted case. Deaths
  and living budget stops retain their denominators.
- **Material direction:** separately for each hero, the same three planned score
  contrasts require net material superiority of at least 0.20 over all ten blocks:
  Immediate Relaxed > Immediate Standard; Survival Standard > Survival Severe;
  Survival Standard > Immediate Standard. The effect is
  `(practical better - practical worse) / 10`, retaining all ties in the denominator;
  it is not a percentage speedup or victory-rate effect.
- **Practical score:** retain exact official score2 counts alongside practical counts.
  Equally complete victories tie only when
  `20 * abs(tA - tB) < max(tA,tB)`; exact 5% remains directional. No time benefit
  accrues to deaths or living stops. There is no significance gate at n=10.
- **Completion floor:** Immediate Relaxed and Survival Standard each win at least
  eight of ten attempts for every hero. Keep all six counts separate.
- **Severe existence:** at least one native Severe Survival/Growth victory per hero
  among ten Survival plus two Growth attempts. This is observed existence only.
- **Unchanged tiers:** all 132 metadata-normalized native journals/full states match
  the adopted baseline; retained differences and failures block that claim.
- **Historical behavior:** native5 fixture reconstruction, accepted continuation,
  atomic rejection, original replay/export/save identity and town recovery remain
