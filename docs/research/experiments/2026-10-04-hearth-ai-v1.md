# Hearth AI v1: first paired experiment

**Result: retain the heuristic baseline.** On twenty reserved seed/hero pairs, sampled formation search finished at mean place **4.65**, versus **4.50** for the baseline (lower is better). Search did better in 8 pairs, tied 4 and did worse in 8. This cohort does not demonstrate a placement improvement.

## Frozen setup

- **Implementation:** clean commit `77dad48f696803b07ebfc1430734c9beab47d5f4`, Hearth rules v5. Both cohorts have the same configuration digest, `ff45d24df7ab7732cd2b37ab779bb703d83f39755b92b78836259e1deba9d57b`.
- **Policies:** `heuristic-v1` and `scout-search-v1`; identical recruitment heuristics, different formation decisions. Both receive the same restricted public observation contract.
- **Search:** at most 24 candidate orders × 16 independent combat samples, conditioned on last-seen scouting. The objective is mean hero damage dealt minus taken. Own end-recruitment effects use native rules; live opponent purchases and environment RNG are excluded.
- **Environment:** seat 0 against seven fixed sequential bots. Five heroes rotate across five development seeds and twenty separate reserved evaluation seeds. Each seed/hero runs both policies. Policy configuration was frozen before evaluation and was not tuned after inspecting it.
- **Evidence:** [machine-readable receipts](2026-10-04-hearth-ai-v1.json) retain every pair and run summary, source/configuration pins, and SHA-256 hashes of the raw replays and decision traces. The subsequent keyword-label UI fix does not change this evaluated engine or either policy.

Evaluation's mean paired placement improvement (baseline minus search) is **−0.15 places**, with sample standard error **0.646 places** across 20 pairs. This is a descriptive uncertainty measure over the cohort, not a population-strength guarantee. Timing covers policy decisions, including cloning the public input; it excludes environment transitions, action enumeration, trace I/O, builds and replay verification. Machine load affects timing.

All **50/50** lobbies completed and reconstructed exactly, with finite-pool invariants checked after every accepted action. No pairs were excluded. A separate artifact audit matched **6,467** decision rows to their step numbers, offered action IDs and replay commands. Both cohorts ran from the same clean source revision. The development rerun retained all five original paired placements.

## Interpretation and next experiment

The candidate completes legal full lobbies, but added search has not earned promotion. Its slightly better top-four count accompanies worse mean placement and fewer first places; selecting only the favorable measure would misstate the result.

Two explanations remain hypotheses: last-seen boards can be poor models of the next fight, and maximizing immediate net damage can differ from maximizing final placement. This experiment does not isolate either cause. Matched initial seeds also diverge in later RNG consumption after different decisions.

The next slice should compare recruitment-policy changes and formation objectives on the development cohort, with explicit compute budgets and opponent-model ablations. A fresh reserved cohort is required for any subsequent strength claim: the twenty evaluation seeds here have now been inspected. Seat rotation and opponent leagues remain future work. These measurements say nothing about human enjoyment or original-game strength.

## Reproduce and inspect

At the evaluated source revision, run:

```sh
npm ci
npm run experiment:hearth -- development test-results/ai/new-development
npm run experiment:hearth -- evaluation test-results/ai/new-evaluation
```

Original local evidence is retained under `test-results/ai/hearth-v1-development-final/` and `test-results/ai/hearth-v1-evaluation/`. These ignored directories contain manifests with individual source hashes, per-decision public inputs/events/search estimates, full replays, receipts and comparisons. Existing output directories are never overwritten. Outcomes and commands are deterministic for this source/configuration; measured times are not.

The [agent contract](../../engineering/hearth-agents.md) documents process requests, observations, legality, persistence and experimental limits.
