# Spire policy v4 development

**Policy mechanics improved; difficulty calibration remains incomplete.** All 240
full development cases completed and passed four audit dimensions. Ironclad's
middle tier still exceeds its easiest tier, so these data do not establish an
ordered ladder. No reserved v4 confirmation has run.

## Trace-driven repairs

The public forecast now reuses native ordered hand effects for Burn and Regret,
accounts for direct health loss separately from block, and processes disclosed
enemy effects in order. Policies preserve energy for a released stasis card and
avoid crediting debuffs after a lethal hit. Survival Ironclad curates rewards,
campfire actions and purchases using offered public commands and card definitions.
Policies receive no seed, hidden draw order or unannounced future intent.

Native rules remain version 3. Independent review checked 320 native parity
fixtures, eight complete historical replays, 1,280 collection decisions and native
menu fixtures. The forecast remains an approximation of future combat phases;
passing these checks does not make it an exact private-state simulator.

## Rejected experiments and evidence limits

Broader power horizons, contextual Fire Breathing, boss-target priorities,
health-reserve and boss-growth variants did not establish a reproducible improvement
on the reused development cohort and were not adopted. One longer-horizon attempt
also crossed a tool-source edit: it remains a source-drift failure, not a valid
frozen study. Local combat correctness alone did not predict full-run strength.

The [receipt](2026-10-16-spire-strength-development-4.json) pins reports and audit
evidence. Original full inputs and bundles remain under
`test-results/spire-strength/`. Exact source bytes for `development-11` were
retained after execution and SHA-verified against its original provenance; this
is historical retention, not a prospective freeze.

Fresh Astra cleared the mechanisms and 20 analyzer checks for a fixed prospective
test, conditional on retaining the complete freeze. It did not predict promotion.
The isolated owned candidate passed maintenance/static/build checks, 534 application
tests and 37 seeded simulations. Actual winning and losing full recordings passed
browser original-input inspection, native seeking, evaluator opt-in, exact download
and 390-pixel overflow checks.
